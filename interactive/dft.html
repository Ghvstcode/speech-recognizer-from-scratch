The Fourier Transform, By Hand

How much of one frequency is in a signal? Multiply the signal by a wave of that frequency, sample by sample, and add up the products. Do that for every frequency and we have the spectrum. That is the whole discrete Fourier transform.

Walk through the six steps on the figure first. Then build your own signal and pick the probe frequency yourself.
