Hearing Like A Human

The FFT gives us 257 numbers per frame, evenly spaced in Hz. Our ears do not space things evenly: they are picky at the bottom and vague at the top. The mel filterbank squeezes the spectrum into 40 numbers the way an ear would, and in doing so it keeps the vowel and loses the pitch.
