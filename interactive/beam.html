Decoding: From Probabilities To Words

The trained network hands us a table: for every frame, a probability for every character and for the blank. Decoding is the job of turning that table back into text. The obvious way is wrong often enough to matter, and the right way is too slow, so we meet in the middle.

Walk through the six steps first. Then change the table and the beam width and see when the answers disagree.
