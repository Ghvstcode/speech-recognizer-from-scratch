Slicing Time

Speech never holds still, so we do not analyze a whole recording at once. We cut it into short overlapping frames and look at each one on its own. The cut itself leaves a mark on the spectrum, and a window function is how we clean it up.

Walk through the five steps on the figure first. Then drag the frame along the signal, change the window, and watch what the spectrum does.
