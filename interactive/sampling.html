Sound Is Just Numbers

A microphone gives us a smoothly wiggling voltage. A WAV file is a list of integers. Two decisions get us from one to the other: how often we measure the wave, and how many bits we spend on each measurement.

Walk through the five steps on the figure first, then move the sliders yourself. The play buttons let you hear what each choice does.

Samples per cycle 36.4 at 440 Hz
Nyquist frequency 8,000 Hz half the sample rate
Alias frequency none everything fits
