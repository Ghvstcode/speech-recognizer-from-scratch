Our First Recognizer: Dynamic Time Warping

Say "seven" twice and the two recordings will not have the same number of frames. Dynamic time warping finds the best way to stretch one onto the other, and tells us how different they still are after the stretching.

Walk through the six steps on the figure first. Then drag the points of the two curves, try the presets, and watch the grid and the path follow.

The two sequences

Changing a length stretches or squeezes the curve, like saying the word slower or faster. To change one frame, drag its dot, or focus the curves and use the arrow keys: left and right pick a dot, up and down move it.

The grid
What each cell holds

Point at a cell, tap it, or focus the grid and move with the arrow keys, to see the three neighbors it was computed from.

The Sakoe-Chiba band

Only cells with |i − j| at most the radius are filled. All the way right turns the band off.
