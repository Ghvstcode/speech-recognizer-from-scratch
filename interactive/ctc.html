The Alignment Problem: CTC

Our network gives one row of probabilities per frame, and our training data gives one short string per clip. Nobody tells us which frame is which letter. CTC gets around that by adding up every way the letters could be laid over the frames.

Walk through the seven steps first. Then edit the table, scrub the lattice, and press Train to watch an alignment appear out of nothing.

Gradient descent

Each step moves every logit against its gradient, with a learning rate of 0.5, as in part 2 of the Rust example. "Forget everything" sets the logits to small random numbers, so every class is equally likely in every frame.
