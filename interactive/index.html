Play With It

Some ideas in the book are easier to believe once we have dragged a slider and watched them happen. Each page here takes one of those ideas and lets us poke at it.

Same maths
The pages compute with the same formulas as the Rust code in the chapters, so what we see here is what our program does.
No install
Plain HTML files. They open from disk as well as from the web, and they load nothing from anywhere else.
Steps first
Every page starts with a short walkthrough on the figure. After that the controls are ours.
Sound
Some pages can play audio. Nothing makes a sound until we press a play button, and the same button stops it.
