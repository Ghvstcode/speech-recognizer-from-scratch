Listening Live

This is the recognizer we trained in chapter 9, the same Rust code, compiled to WebAssembly and running in this page. Speak, or pick a recording, and it finds the speech, runs the network and spells out what it heard.

It knows 35 words and nothing else, and it learned them from one dataset of one-second clips. Say two or three of them with a short pause in between. Expect mistakes with your own voice and microphone: chapter 10 measures how often it is wrong even on its own test set. Your audio never leaves this page.
